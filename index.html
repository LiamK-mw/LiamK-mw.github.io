Pickleball Scorekeeper

A Wear OS app: tap who won the rally, and your watch keeps score and calls it out loud.

  • Traditional side-out scoring (0-0-2), doubles or singles, optional rally scoring
  • Shows who serves and from which side
  • Spoken score, vibrations, one-tap undo
  • Optional workout tracking: heart rate, calories and time
