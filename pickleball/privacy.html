Privacy Policy

Pickleball Scorekeeper · Effective November 12, 2026

Pickleball Scorekeeper ("the app") is a Wear OS app made by Liam Kelly. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on your watch

The app stores your current match, your finished matches (scores and times) and your settings on your watch so it can keep your score and show your history. This information never leaves your watch. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Workout data (heart rate and calories)

If "Track workout" is on, the app uses your watch's built-in fitness service (Wear OS Health Services) to record each match as a workout: your heart rate, calories burned and how long you played. This is only used to show your heart rate during the match and a workout summary in your match history. It is stored only on your watch, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can turn workout tracking off in the app's Settings, or deny the heart rate permission, and the app still keeps score normally.

Text-to-speech

To call the score out loud, the app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your watch (for example Google's speech engine). The spoken text is only the score and similar game information.

Permissions

The app uses vibration to confirm taps, and it keeps the screen on while it is open. It may raise the media volume while open so you can hear the score, and restores it when you leave. For workout tracking it asks for permission to read your heart rate and physical activity, and to show a notification that lets you return to a match in progress.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
